🌍 PART 1 — WORLD LORE: THE CHRONICLES OF ELTHERIS

1.1 The Shattered Sky & The Wellsprings of Aether

Six centuries ago, the continent of Eltheris was nearly unmade during the Cataclysm of Falling Stars. Sorcerer-kings tore open the leylines of the world to harvest Aether—the primordial celestial energy that fuels high sorcery, grand metallurgy, and superhuman martial mastery. Because Aether conducts most potently through living skin attuned to runic silk and conductive mythril-alloy, the elite champions of Eltheris eschew bulky, restrictive full-body armor in favor of high-conductivity Aether-Weave Regalia—sculpted half-cuirasses, runic corsets, sheer silk vestments, and garter-anchored greaves that maximize mobility and arcane resonance.

When the crust fractured at the geographic heart of Eltheris, an ancient void stirred in the depths: The Hollow, an encroaching oblivion that consumes vitality, memory, and territorial cohesion.

To prevent the total collapse of their lands, two rival rulers—the Solar Sovereign of Aurelis and the Shadow Emperor of Umbrenholt—forged the First Concord. Over the yawning abyss, they anchored sixty-four colossal monolith slabs of alternating Dawnstone (radiant white marble infused with solar quartz) and Duskstone (polished, starlit obsidian): The Thronefield.

1.2 The Law of the Sixty-Four Thrones

Under the ancient oaths of the First Concord, total continental war is bound by ancient runic treaties. When border crises or disputes over the remaining Aether Wellsprings erupt, the courts convene the Rite of Thrones:

Sixteen Champions per Court: Each realm deploys its Sovereign (King), Grand Marshal/Regent (Queen), two Bastion Wardens (Rooks), two Astral/Umbral Seers (Bishops—scholarly astronomers, arcanists, and illusionists with zero religious clergy affiliations), two Mounted Cavaliers (Knights), and eight Vanguard Sworn (Pawns).

The Geas of Movement: Stepping onto the Thronefield binds each warrior's stride to geometric leylines:

Wardens (Rooks) charge in straight, crushing vectors of kinetic force.

Seers (Bishops) channel diagonal beams of refracted starlight or shadow along ley intersections.

Cavaliers (Knights) traverse the field in soaring dimensional leaps over obstructions.

Queens wield mastery over both linear kinetic force and diagonal sorcery, serving as the realm's apex military tacticians.

The Stasis of "The Quiet": When a piece is defeated, arcane overload shatters their outer Aether-barrier—tearing away armor plating and scorching silk vestments—before drawing them into temporal suspended animation beneath the stone, a state known as The Quiet. For centuries, the masked arbiter of the monoliths claimed the Unthroned rest in peaceful preservation until the tournament ends.

The Uncovered Threat: In truth, the ancient seal holding back the Hollow has degraded. The Quiet is steadily leaking vitality into the void beneath. If the champions do not discover the truth and unite across factional lines, the fallen will be permanently lost to the void.

1.3 Magic & Steel: Lumen vs. Umbra

Every engagement on the board manifests as a clash of high-fantasy sorcery and swordsmanship:

Lumen (House Aurelis — White): Radiant kinetic energy derived from solar prism light. Champions channel golden wards, incandescent blades, and blinding speed, leaving lingering ribbons of solar dawn across sun-kissed skin and white-and-gold regalia.

Umbra (Umbrenholt Dominion — Black): Gravitational and twilight energy drawn from celestial night. Champions manipulate density, spatial folds, and shadow-forged steel, commanding cool violet mists that cling to obsidian corsetry and sheer midnight lace.

2.1 The War Tent Hub (Between Every Battle)

Before taking the field, the Commander manages logistical preparation, spending 2\text{ Evening Action Points (AP)}:

Counsel Quarters (1\text{ AP} — Strategic Fellowship & Bond Ranks):

Meet with an officer in their private pavilion for counsel, weapon tuning, or tactical dialogue. In camp, officers appear in State C: Camp Attire (glamorous, revealing off-duty loungewear—unbuttoned silk shirts, cropped underboob-baring camisoles, short pleated lounge skirts, and tight stay-up stockings).

Advancing through 4 Fellowship Ranks unlocks character lore, tactical bonuses, and battle insights:

Rank 1 (10\text{ Affinity} — Acquaintance): Unlocks background lore, homeland history, and their entry in the Codex Dossier.

Rank 2 (25\text{ Affinity} — Trusted Confidant): The officer shares their core motivation, fallen comrades, or personal misgivings about the Rite. Unlocks Enhanced Advisor Analysis (in-depth tactical rationale behind their suggested chess moves).

Rank 3 (45\text{ Affinity} — Sworn Comrade): A major character scene centered on shared battlefield trust, philosophical alignment, or private sparring. Unlocks +1\text{ Max Aether Charge} during matches.

Rank 4 (70\text{ Affinity} — Champion's Oath): Unlocks a widescreen, high-glamour Oath of Allegiance CG on the eve of Chapter 8, enabling their unique Campaign Conclusion Epilogue.

War Table Reconnaissance (1\text{ AP} — Tactical Recon):

Consult with your Seer (Ilyara or Veyra) as she leans over the illuminated war-table to analyze the enemy commander's AI Flaw & Opening Tendencies, granting +1\text{ Bonus Aether Charge} in the next battle.

The Archive of Records (0\text{ AP} — Free Action):

Review battle logs, intercepted letters, unlocked CGs, character wardrobe state viewers, and diplomatic lore.

5.1 The Heuristic Evaluation Function (game/core/story_ai.py)

Piece values in centipawns:

V = \{ \text{Pawn}: 100,\ \text{Knight}: 310,\ \text{Bishop}: 330,\ \text{Rook}: 500,\ \text{Queen}: 900,\ \text{King}: 0 \}

For any legal move m \in \text{Board.legal\_moves}, evaluation components are defined deterministically:

C(m): Centipawn value of captured target (V(\text{captured\_piece}), or 100 if board.is_en_passant(m), else 0).

K(m): +150 if move m delivers Check (board.gives_check(m)), else 0.

D(m): Central board control (+30 if m.to_square has file f \in \{2,3,4,5\} and rank r \in \{2,3,4,5\}, else 0).

R(m): Risk penalty (-0.8 \times V(\text{moving\_piece}) if after board.push(m), board.is_attacked_by(enemy_color, m.to_square) is True and board.is_attacked_by(friendly_color, m.to_square) is False; else 0).

F(m): Tactical fork/pin bonus (+180 if after board.push(m), the moved piece on m.to_square attacks \ge 2 enemy pieces of value \ge 310; else 0).

G(m): King safety score (+120 if board.is_castling(m) is True, or if m.to_square is Chebyshev distance \le 2 from the friendly King square, or if a friendly piece captures an attacker of the King zone; else 0).

The 3 Distinct War Council Advisor Formulas

To ensure your advisors offer diverse, characterful strategic options, three distinct evaluation functions are computed:

The Marshal's Strike (Lysandra / Nyssara — Tactical Offense, Red Vector):

S_{\text{Queen}}(m) = 1.8 \cdot C(m) + 2.0 \cdot K(m) + 1.2 \cdot D(m) + 0.4 \cdot R(m)

(Emphasizes proactive pressure, development, checks, and decisive trades.)

The Bastion's Guard (Brannoch / Dravok — Defense & Fortification, Blue Vector):

S_{\text{Warden}}(m) = 1.5 \cdot G(m) + 1.0 \cdot D(m) + 0.8 \cdot C(m) + 2.5 \cdot R(m)

(Prioritizes King safety, structure reinforcement, avoiding blunders, and solid trades.)

The Seer's Path (Ilyara / Veyra — Tactical Geometry & Forks, Violet Vector):

S_{\text{Seer}}(m) = 2.2 \cdot F(m) + 1.4 \cdot D(m) + 1.1 \cdot C(m) + 1.2 \cdot R(m)

(Specializes in pins, multi-target threats, diagonal pressure, and unexpected angles.)

Strict Uniqueness Filter (With Legal-Move Count Fallback): Let M = \text{list}(\text{get\_filtered\_legal\_moves}()).

If |M| == 0: return [].

m_{\text{Queen}} = \arg\max_{m \in M} S_{\text{Queen}}(m)

m_{\text{Warden}} = \arg\max_{m \in M \setminus \{m_{\text{Queen}}\}} S_{\text{Warden}}(m) (or m_{\text{Queen}} if |M| < 2)

m_{\text{Seer}} = \arg\max_{m \in M \setminus \{m_{\text{Queen}}, m_{\text{Warden}}\}} S_{\text{Seer}}(m) (or fallback if |M| < 3)

5.3 Sovereign Spells & Sanitized python-chess Rules (game/core/match_session.py)

To prevent any python-chess state corruption or illegal bitboard states, all 4 Sovereign Spells adhere to strict validation rules:

Chrono-Pulse (Cost: 1\text{ Aether} — Tactical Rewind):

Guard: Enabled only when len(fen_history_list) >= 2 and it is the player's turn.

Execution: Pops the last 2 half-move snapshots (fen, spell_state_snapshot) from fen_history_list and reconstructs board = chess.Board(restored_fen), cleanly restoring active wards and turn indicators without conflicting with Ren'Py rollback.

Oracle's Gaze (Cost: 1\text{ Aether} — Foresight):

Guard: Enabled on the player's turn when oracle_active == False.

Execution: Clones board.copy(), runs the current Boss AI evaluation deterministically (\sigma = 0) to predict the opponent's highest-priority threat response, and highlights the predicted origin and destination squares in pulsing gold/violet.

Aegis Ward (Cost: 2\text{ Aether} — Bastion Shield):

Guard: Player selects one friendly non-King piece (piece.piece_type != chess.KING and piece.color == player_color).

Execution: Stores warded_square = target_sq for 1 enemy turn.

Wrapper Safety Rule (get_filtered_legal_moves(board)):

If warded_square is not None and board.turn != player_color:

First check if the piece on warded_square is currently delivering check to the enemy King (warded_square in board.checkers()) OR if the enemy King is in check and capturing warded_square is required. If board.is_check() is True, Aegis Ward immunity is automatically bypassed so python-chess check evasion rules remain 100\% valid.

Otherwise, filter out moves where m.to_square == warded_square. If the filtered move list is empty, fall back to unfiltered board.legal_moves to prevent an artificial stalemate.

If the player moves the warded piece on a subsequent step, warded_square clears automatically.

Marshal's Advance (Cost: 2\text{ Aether} — Rapid Deployment):

Guard:

Cannot be cast while the player's King is currently in Check (not board.is_check()).

Target piece must be a friendly Pawn (piece.piece_type == chess.PAWN) located on 0-indexed ranks 1 \le r \le 5 (so the 1-square advance never lands on the promotion rank r=0 or r=7).

The single-step forward move step_move = chess.Move(sq, sq + step_offset) must be in board.legal_moves, must be a non-capture (board.piece_at(sq + step_offset) is None), and must not deliver check (not board.gives_check(step_move)).

Execution: Executes board.push(step_move) followed immediately by board.push(chess.Move.null()). Because step_move is verified not to give check and the player was not in check, chess.Move.null() is guaranteed 100\% legal in python-chess and returns the turn to the player!

5.4 Dynamic Sprite State Switching (game/core/event_detector.py)

Each turn, event_detector.py computes the net material balance in pawns (\Delta M = \frac{M_{\text{side}} - M_{\text{opponent}}}{100}) and Check status for both the Player Commander and the Opponent Boss:

Transition to state_b_worn (Battle-Damaged / Revealing State):

Triggered immediately for a side when \Delta M \le -2.0 OR board.is_check() is True for that side's King OR that side's Queen has been captured.

When transitioning from state_a_battle \rightarrow state_b_worn, triggers the character's Action Cut-In Banner (cutins/{char_id}_cutin.png) with an armor-shatter SFX and a screen shake (vpunch).

Hysteresis Recovery to state_a_battle:

Recovers to state_a_battle only if the side is not in Check, still retains their Queen, and pushes material balance back to \Delta M \ge 0.0 (preventing rapid flickering between -1.0 and -2.0).
